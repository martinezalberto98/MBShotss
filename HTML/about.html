About

Miggs Borromeo

San Diego Photographer

Miggs Borromeo is a photographer who has a natural eye and special feel for the angles when it comes to pictures and videos. A curious individual who is driven and motivated to learn what it takes to get to the next level of photography. He is a visionary and content creator that founded MBshotss_.

MBshotss_ is an upcoming brand that focuses on capturing each moment one shot at a time. It specializes in DJ Sets, Boat/Rooftop Parties, and Fitness Content, but adaptable enough to work outside the comfort zone. The brand promises to deliver dynamic, high quality and energetic content. It's the next big thing.

  • Specialties: DJ Events, Fitness, Lifestyle, Boat Charters
